Questions

Frequently asked

1. Do I need to be a member to book a private?
No. Privates are available to members and non-members.
2. Can I bring a training partner?
Yes. You can bring up to 3 additional people. The price is $100 for the first person and $20 per extra person.
3. What if I'm a complete beginner?
That's fine. Privates are a great way to start. We'll cover the fundamentals at your own pace.
4. Do I need to wear anything specific?
Rash guard or fitted t-shirt and shorts with no zippers or pockets. Clean gear and trimmed nails.
5. Can I book a private on the same day?
Subject to availability. Best to book at least 24 hours in advance.
