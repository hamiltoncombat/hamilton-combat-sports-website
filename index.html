What we teach

A complete system that gives you effective standing and ground skills for self-defence, fitness and competition.

GRAPPLING

Grappling is the modern fighting system focused on positional control, transitions and finishing with joint locks or chokes. It draws from SAMBO, Judo, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, wrestling and catch wrestling. Ideal for building functional strength, timing and real-world control skills.

  • Positional dominance & escapes
  • Submissions (chokes, joint locks)
  • Live sparring / rolling

Skill Development

Learn effective takedowns, positional control, escapes and submissions through structured coaching. Every class builds on previous lessons so beginners and experienced students continue to improve.

Pressure Tested

Develop practical skills that work under pressure. Training focuses on controlling an opponent, escaping disadvantageous positions and applying proven grappling techniques safely and effectively.

Competition Ready

Whether your goal is local tournaments or higher-level competition, classes include live drilling, positional sparring and coaching strategies that prepare you for real competition.

Common questions

Frequently asked

1. What are the uniform requirements for class?
  • Grappling: Rash guard or fitted t-shirt and shorts (no zippers or pockets).
  • All gear must be clean and nails trimmed for hygiene and safety.
2. Do I need to be fit to start?
No. Everyone starts somewhere. Classes are structured so you build fitness as you learn technique. Go at your own pace.
3. How old do I need to be?
Classes combine adults and teenagers aged 14 and over. The $15 casual rate applies to ages 14–15; members aged 16+ use the adult rate. Anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian to sign a waiver.
4. Will I get injured?
We prioritise safety. Warm-ups, supervised drilling, and no sparring without coaching approval. Minor bruises can happen; serious injury is rare.
5. Do you spar / roll on the first night?
No. Your first few sessions focus on basic movements, breakfalls and positional drills. Sparring is optional and only after you're comfortable.
6. What if I have no grappling experience?
That's normal. Most members started as beginners. The free intro class covers the absolute basics – no prior knowledge needed.
7. Can I just watch a class before joining?
Yes. You're welcome to observe a session. Just message ahead so we know you're coming.
8. What if I can't make it every week?
That's fine. Train when you can. There is no attendance requirement or contract lock-in.
9. Do you have changing rooms or showers?
The venue has changing facilities and showers. Most students arrive ready to train.
10. Can I bring a friend to the free trial?
Yes. They can also book a free trial. Just let us know in advance so we have space.
